TikTok Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview

TikTok Uploader ("the App") is a personal desktop application for Windows that publishes the user's own videos to their own TikTok accounts via the official TikTok API. This policy explains what data the App handles.

2. Data the App receives from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives:

  • your TikTok open ID, username and display name;
  • access and refresh tokens needed to upload and publish videos on your behalf.

The App does not receive or store your TikTok password.

3. How data is stored

  • All data (account tokens, settings, upload queue, logs) is stored only locally on the user's computer, in the %APPDATA%\TikTokUploader folder.
  • The App has no servers. No data is sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok through the official API when you choose to publish them.

4. How data is used

Data is used solely to upload and publish videos to the TikTok accounts you have connected, and to show upload status in the App. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling, and is never sold or shared.

5. Data deletion

  • Removing an account in the App deletes its tokens from your computer.
  • Deleting the %APPDATA%\TikTokUploader folder removes all App data.
  • You can also revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

6. Children

The App is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
