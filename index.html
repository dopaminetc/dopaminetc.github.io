TikTok Uploader

Last updated: October 10, 2026

TikTok Uploader is a personal Windows desktop application for publishing your own videos to your own TikTok accounts — immediately, on a schedule, or from a watched folder — using the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

  • Connect several TikTok accounts via official TikTok login (the App never sees your password)
  • Upload one video to multiple accounts with progress tracking
  • Schedule posts and set intervals between videos
  • All data is stored locally on your computer
